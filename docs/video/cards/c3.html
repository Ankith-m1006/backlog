how it works

From chat to answer, with you in the loop.

01

Read

Twitch EventSub streams every chat message in real time.

02

Filter

Emotes, banter and spam are dropped. A model keeps only real questions.

03

Merge

Different wordings of the same question stack on one ticket.

04

Rank

Most asked and most recent first. Pin or dismiss with one key.

05

Reply

A saved answer matches. You click Send. The bot replies in chat.
