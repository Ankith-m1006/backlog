# Backlog: demo voiceover (read this, record as one MP3)

Read at a calm, normal pace. Each block matches what is on screen; the time is where the block starts. If you finish early, just pause before the next one. I fit the video to your audio afterwards. Total about 2 min 25 s.

---

**[0:00, title card]**
This is Backlog. Your Twitch chat asks the same question forty times. Backlog asks you once.

**[0:10, the problem]**
When you stream, chat moves fast. The real questions scroll away in seconds, the same question gets asked again and again, and the new viewer who asked it once never gets an answer.

**[0:24, live: my Twitch chat on the left, Backlog on the right]**
This is my real channel. On the left is Twitch chat. On the right is Backlog's control room. As messages come in, Backlog reads them through Twitch EventSub. "LUL" and "GG that was clean" are not questions, so they're ignored. You can see the counter at the top: chat, questions, ignored.

**[0:50, the keyboard ticket]**
I asked about my keyboard three different ways. Backlog stacked all three on one ticket, and you can open it to see every wording. It also found an answer I saved earlier.

**[1:01, Send, bot replies]**
I click Send. A second later, my bot account replies in chat with my saved answer.

**[1:09, how it works]**
Here's the flow. Read chat. Filter out emotes and banter. A model keeps only real questions. Merge repeats into one ticket. Rank by how many people asked and how recently. And reply, only when I click Send.

**[1:25, busy chat replay]**
This is a replay of a busy four-hundred-message chat, classified live. Most messages are noise. The questions rise to the top, ranked by asks, with tally marks so I can read them at a glance mid-game.

**[1:50, OBS overlay]**
There's also an overlay for OBS. Viewers see the top three questions on stream, so they know they've been heard.

**[2:02, what it never does]**
Backlog never writes an answer and never sends on its own. The bot only sends words I saved, after I click Send, at most one message every ten seconds.

**[2:16, end card]**
Backlog is open source. Every question gets its turn.
