# YouTube

## Title
Backlog: Twitch chat asks 40 times, you answer once (Codex x TwitchCon demo)

## Description
Backlog is an open-source question queue for Twitch streamers. It reads chat live, drops emotes and banter, merges different wordings of the same question into one ticket, ranks them by how many people asked, and lets the streamer answer with a saved reply in one click.

It never writes answers and never sends on its own: the bot only posts words the streamer saved, after the streamer clicks Send, at most one message every 10 seconds.
